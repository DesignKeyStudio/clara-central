# Licensing

Clara Central is **source-available**. You can read the code, run it, and change it, but the free
license covers **non-commercial use only**. Commercial use needs a paid license from Design Key LLC.

This page is a plain-language summary. The [license](./LICENSE.md) is the binding text. Where this
page and the license differ, the license wins.

## Free: PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0

The code in this repository is licensed under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](./LICENSE.md). You can use Clara Central free of charge if
you are:

- **A noncommercial organization.** The license names charitable organizations, educational
  institutions, public research organizations, public safety or health organizations, environmental
  protection organizations, and government institutions. This applies however the organization is
  funded.
- **An individual using it personally,** with no anticipated commercial application. That covers
  personal study, research, experimentation and testing, and hobby projects.

Under the free license you may run the software, modify it, and share copies. Anyone who receives
a copy from you must also receive the license terms and the `Required Notice:` line at the top of
[LICENSE.md](./LICENSE.md).

## Paid: the commercial license

Any other use needs a commercial license. Common cases:

| Your situation | What you need |
|---|---|
| You are a charity, school, research body, or government institution running your own referral program | Nothing. The free license covers you. |
| You are a business (agency, consultancy, SaaS company, or any other) running your own referral or partner program on Clara Central | A commercial license. This applies even if you never charge anyone for it and only use it internally. |
| You are a freelancer or agency installing, customizing, or hosting Clara Central for a business client | A commercial license that covers the client. Talk to us before you start. |
| You want to resell Clara Central, bundle it into a product you sell, or run it as a hosted service for other companies | This is not permitted under the free license, and it is not part of the standard commercial license. It needs a separate written agreement with us. |
| You want to see whether Clara Central fits your business | Try the [live demo](https://app.claracentral.com/demo). It needs no install and no signup. To trial a self-hosted copy before buying, contact us. |

### Buying a license

The commercial license is a one-time fee. Current pricing is on
[claracentral.com](https://www.claracentral.com/#pricing).

To buy a license, or to ask whether your use counts as commercial, email
[info@designkey.studio](mailto:info@designkey.studio) or use the
[contact form](https://www.claracentral.com/#contact). If you would rather not run the software
yourself, we also offer a managed deployment where we host and maintain it for you.

### Not sure which applies?

Ask us before you deploy. We would much rather answer a question than chase a licensing problem
later, and we cannot tell you after the fact that an unlicensed commercial deployment was fine.

## What these licenses do not cover

- **Third-party code keeps its own license.** Components copied in from shadcn/ui and ReUI remain
  under their MIT licenses, and packages installed from npm keep theirs. See
  [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](./THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).
- **The name and logos are not licensed.** Neither license grants any right to the Clara Central
  name or the logos in `public/`. If you share a modified copy, remove them and use your own.
